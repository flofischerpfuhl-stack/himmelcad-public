  are green, but hardware/file-format throughput was not measured.

  second raw-RPC allowlist or broaden user-only approval/credential surfaces.

## S-05b — G17 corrections and cross-product chip convergence

Date: 2026-09-04

- The washed-out **Respond** label was caused by the jobs-island descendant
  selector `.copy span`, which crossed the shared Button boundary and replaced
  `--hc-fg-on-accent` with `--hc-fg-muted`. The row phase now has its own class,
  so Button typography and foreground remain component-owned. The compact
  action uses the shared `--hc-accent-aa-fill` token because the prior accent
  fill measured only 3.12:1 against white; the gallery runner now rejects
  Respond text/fill below 4.5:1 in either theme.
- Unknown-unit rows render their real phase, **in progress**, and the shared
  indeterminate ProgressBar. Its jobs track is 4 px and its 30% segment sweeps
  linearly in 1.2 s; no determinate `aria-valuenow` or synthetic 0% is emitted.
- `JobsStatusChip` now implements the UIP-D10 revision: a 20 px, UI-11 pill,
  state-tone borders/text, a 14 px spinner or 48×3 px determinate bar, the H2
  single/multi/input/cancelling/failure/completion grammar, `Jobs: <label>`
  accessible naming, and tunable 4 s completion linger. Builder places it
  after Panel toggles and clicking it toggles the jobs island. PhotoLab source
  was reference-only and was not edited.
- Shared `OverlayChip` viewport chrome now uses token-only `island-hi`,
  `border-default`, and `fg-default` styling; its active state uses the
  design-system accent-outline pattern with `fg-strong`. The new **Viewport
  chrome** gallery section covers the 3D/2.5D/2D control and `X — Y — Z —`
  chip, with rendered-pixel text/fill checks at 4.5:1 in both themes.
