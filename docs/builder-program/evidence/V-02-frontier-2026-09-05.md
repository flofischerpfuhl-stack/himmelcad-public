## Doctrine decisions

> **Decision:** Prepared point metadata schema version 1 records, per node,
> geometric error, point spacing, exact sampled/source counts when known, sample
> method when known, station ids when declared, payload SHA-256 when known, and
> metadata origin. It separately records the raw source SHA-256. Unknown data is
> `null`, never inferred.
> **Derivation:** X2, P2, VC-D2 and ADR 0003 make preprocessing the place to buy
> interaction speed while retaining raw source authority and unchanged Potree
> files.
> **Rejected:** rewriting Potree `metadata.json`, treating sampled display data
> as authority, or inventing station/source-count/method fields.
> **Tunable:** no for hashes, provenance and unknown-value semantics; schema
> evolution is versioned.

> **Decision:** Existing Potree 2 datasets take the unchanged parser entry point.
> As hierarchy pages arrive, spacing/error and sampled count are derived exactly
> from Potree spacing, level and hierarchy count and cached in descriptors.
> Missing payload hashes are computed once from fetched bytes and cached by tile
> generation. Unknown station ids, source counts and sample methods stay absent.
> **Derivation:** ADR 0003's unchanged-format compatibility plus X1 correctness
> and VC-D2's raw-authority rule.
> **Rejected:** eager whole-octree hashing and placeholder provenance.
> **Tunable:** no.

> **Decision:** The selector orders by projected physical-pixel error discounted
> by distance from viewport center, then front-to-back depth and stable key.
> Admission uses weighted virtual finish across datasets, so projected error
> controls proportional share without arrival-order dependence.
> **Derivation:** VC-D3, §1.2 center-out refinement and X1/P4 visible-set scope.
> **Rejected:** strict dataset priority, arrival order and equal shares that
> ignore projected error.
> **Tunable:** the priority weighting is tunable; fair progress and stable
> deterministic ties are not.

> **Decision:** A hard render-frontier pass may remove ADD detail only while a
> resident parent remains, or atomically collapse resident REPLACE descendants
> to a resident parent. The sole covering fallback is never removed. Selected
> costs and limiting dimensions are returned on every plan.
> **Derivation:** VC-D3, VC-D8 and §1.2 continuity under X1 correctness.
> **Rejected:** dropping arbitrary tiles to meet a scalar cap or evicting the
> last coarse coverage.
> **Tunable:** numeric budgets are tunable under X6/P3; continuity is not.
