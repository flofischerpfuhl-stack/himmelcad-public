# S-04 selection substrate evidence — 2026-09-04

## Outcome

Builder now has one selection store of record in `@himmelcad/app`. The tree,
viewport, Properties surface, `view.state`, and the canonical `select.*`
automation rows all read or mutate that owner. Selection is local state: its
history is independent from the document journal and is persisted per project
as the `selection` instance of `hcad.local-history@1`.

- The UIP-D6 runtime command/context-menu registry is not present; S-04 exposes
  the exact candidate contribution for S-06 rather than creating a second menu
  mechanism.
- The current registry has no assigned shortcuts for selection-local undo/redo.
  The canonical `select.undo`/`select.redo` commands are registered and Ctrl+Z
  was deliberately not captured; no unapproved shortcut was invented.
- Automated pointer tests cover mouse and emulated touch semantics. Real touch
  hardware was not available in this pass.
- The store supplies the required cloud/splat bounding-box-halo treatment flag;
  a full Builder scene screenshot of that renderer treatment was not possible
  without a live cloud/splat project. The shared surface shots above were
  rendered and inspected in both themes.
